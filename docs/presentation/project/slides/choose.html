So how do you choose? Ask yourselves three questions. Do we know exactly where every patch is, and do we just want the fastest run? Then Basic functions. Are the patches along the walls, and do we want autonomy points without much risk? Then the wall follower. Could the patches be anywhere, or must they be found in a set order? Then the maze explorer. Two more things. Whichever you choose, you can still write your own moves, before, after or around the autonomy: for example, drive to a good starting point and then hand over. And the software is part of your budget, so weigh it against what else you could spend that money on.
