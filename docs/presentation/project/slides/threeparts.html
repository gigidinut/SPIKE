Your program file is long, over a thousand lines, but don’t panic: to get your rover going, you only need to change three places. The SETTINGS near the top, where you describe your rover and your course. YOUR PROGRAM at the very bottom, where you choose what runs or write your own moves. And on_colour_found, in the middle: search the file for ADD YOUR OWN ACTIONS HERE; that’s where you say what the rover does on each colour. Everything else is the rover software, and tinkering is encouraged. Read it, play with it, change it: that’s the best way to understand how your rover works. Just save a copy that works first, so you can always go back.
