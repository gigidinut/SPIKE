Rover software 1.1 · 13

This is the simulator. It runs your real program file, the same one you put on the hub, with a pretend rover on a pretend course. You draw your rover, its wheels and sensors, and your course, its walls and patches. When you run, you see the rover’s path, and a log of everything the program printed. Click any line and the simulator shows you where the rover was at that moment. And if the settings in your program don’t match the rover you drew, you get a red warning. That one matters: in one of our tests, a rover running with the wrong wheel size got completely lost. The simulator is a model, so always test on the real rover too, but it saves you hours on the floor.
