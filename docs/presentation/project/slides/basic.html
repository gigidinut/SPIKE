Rover software 1.1 · 7

Package one: Basic functions. You write the route as a list of moves, like this: drive 60 centimetres, face west, drive on until you’re 15 centimetres from the wall, face north, drive 80 more. The software makes every one of those moves accurate. It’s great if you know the course well, and it gives you the fastest run. The catch: anything you didn’t plan for breaks the route. My tip: plan your route along walls. Driving up to a wall corrects the small errors from the moves before it, because the wall is always where it is.
