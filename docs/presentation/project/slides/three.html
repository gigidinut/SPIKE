So here’s the choice you have to make as a team. There are three packages. With Basic functions, you plan the route and the rover drives it accurately. With the Wall follower, the rover finds its own way by following the walls. With the Maze explorer, the rover draws a map of the course and searches all of it. Each one costs a different amount from your budget, and each one has strengths and weaknesses. Let’s compare them.
