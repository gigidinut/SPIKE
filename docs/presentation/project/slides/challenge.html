Rover software 1.1 · 2

Here’s the challenge. Your rover has to get through the course, find the coloured patches and do something at each one. You know the layout, so you could hard-code the route: drive 60, turn left, drive 40, and so on. But a rover that finds its own way scores more points. The trouble is, autonomy is hard. In past years most teams gave up on it and hard-coded the route. This year you don’t have to write autonomy from scratch: you can buy it, as software.
