Rover software 1.1 · 14

A word about design, before you build. The software turns the rover on the spot, by running one side forwards and the other backwards. So you need driven wheels on both sides; a rover that steers like a car can’t run the autonomy. When the rover spins, its corners sweep a circle: about 27 centimetres across for a rover 15 by 18. Gaps narrower than that are no-go zones, so a compact rover gets through more of the course. Four or six wheels work fine; they slide a bit when turning, so turns are slower, and two of the setup tests measure that for the simulator. And put the sensors where they can do their job: the distance sensor pointing straight ahead at the walls, and the colour sensor facing the floor, close to it.
