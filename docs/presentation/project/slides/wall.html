Package 2 · Wall follower

One hand on the wall, all the way round

  1. Drive straight on until there is a wall ahead.
  2. Turn so the wall is on your chosen hand.
  3. At every square, try: towards the hand, straight on, away from the hand, back.
  4. Stop when back at the very first move.

Good for

Simple and predictable. Needs no map. Finds patches next to walls.

Watch out

Misses patches in the open, and walls not joined to the rest.

Blue: the rover’s path (right hand on the wall). It finds the green patch by the wall, and misses the red one in the open.

Package two: the wall follower. It works the way you could find your way round a maze in the dark: keep one hand on the wall and keep walking. The rover drives straight on until it meets a wall, turns so the wall is on its chosen hand, and then at every step tries to turn towards that hand, then straight on, then away, then back. It stops when it’s about to repeat its very first move: it has been all the way round. It’s simple, it needs no map, and it’s easy to predict. But look at the picture: it finds the green patch by the wall, and it never goes near the red patch in the middle of the floor. You choose which hand in the settings, so try both in the simulator with your course.
