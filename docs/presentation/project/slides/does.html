Rover software 1.1 · 3

Whatever you buy, the software does four things for you. One: it drives straight and exact distances. The hub’s gyro steers the rover back if it drifts, and the motors count how far the wheels turned. Two: it turns to exact directions. NORTH is the way your rover faces at the start, and the directions stay fixed to the room, so small errors don’t add up turn after turn. Three: the distance sensor stops a drive before it hits a wall, and tells the autonomy where the walls are. Four: the colour sensor watches the floor during every single move, and when it finds a colour it runs the actions you wrote.
