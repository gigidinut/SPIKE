Maze explorer

Here are all three on the same course. With Basic functions, the rover goes exactly where you planned. It finds both patches, but only because you knew where they were. The wall follower goes round the walls: it finds the green patch in the corner and never reaches the red one in the middle. The explorer covers every square it can reach, so it finds both, but it takes the longest. None of them is the best. The best one depends on your course and on how sure you are about where the patches are.
