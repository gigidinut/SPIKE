    Rover as JSON - to save it, or share it with your team

    Measure from any point you like; the presets use the middle of the drive wheels. body is a list of boxes [x0, x1, y0, y1]. Each wheel has x, y and diameter d; a driven wheel also has the motor's port, forward (the speed sign that rolls it forward, 1 or -1) and, if it is geared, gear (motor turns per wheel turn). Wheels that swivel, and ball casters, have "caster": true. Sensors have type ("distance" or "colour"), port, x, y and, for a sensor that points sideways, angle (degrees, + is to the left).

    Each wall is [x0, x1, y0, y1]. Green, red and blue are the same shape, or null. Start is [x, y]; the rover starts facing up.
